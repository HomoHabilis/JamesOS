# Plaits comparison defaults

Turning the Presets knob on a synth track loads one clean starting point per Plaits 1.2 model, in hardware bank order: orange, green, red. Each uses the main OUT (OSC MIX 0), open JamesOS filter, no sends or modulation, and a quick amplitude envelope. Harmonics, Timbre and Morph are 0–127 values, not hardware measurements. Grain Cloud uses full Morph and moderate density/randomization as suggested in the manual. The drum starting points are kick H/T/M 24/48/64, snare 80/64/52, and hi-hat 80/100/24: respectively less kick drive, more snare noise, and a brighter, shorter hat. Hardware tuning, trigger/level patching, LPG settings and JamesOS envelopes affect the comparison; adjust by ear on the actual devices.

The original Plaits panel has 16 icons. The 1.2 orange bank shares the eight green positions on the panel, so those eight square badges reuse the corresponding symbol in orange; green and red retain their panel colors. Icons are adapted (resized, converted to monochrome and shown in colored squares) from the [Mutable Instruments Plaits manual](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/plaits/manual/), by Mutable Instruments, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The 1.2 models are described in the [firmware notes](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/plaits/firmware/).

Existing sound slots and session IDs remain unchanged. Turning Presets resets the model controls, output mix, filter, envelope, sends and modulation to the comparison default while preserving the track level, pan, tuning, pattern and routing.
